We are a specialist manufacturer of road-facility safety products that pursues management innovation and customer satisfaction, standardized operations, technological leadership and advanced, human-centered management values.

With infrastructure and skilled engineers covering every process from manufacturing to installation, we have adopted a low-power smart battery system optimized for the road environment to produce a wide range of road-safety products.
